VIP etiquette

You're the talent too. There will be a lot of high-profile celebrities in the house, and you belong in that room. Be fun, be confident and be fabulous. Say hi, share the vibe and enjoy the moment, then let it flow. Nobody here is a fan chasing a photo. We're all VIPs together.

Every star arrives with a team that protects their privacy, and we respect it. If someone's team says "not right now," smile, step back, and catch the next moment. Following the protocol is how we all keep the night beautiful and get invited back. And remember, the VIP Creator Lounge is your stage. That's where you take command, have fun and shine.

  • 18+ for the Creator Lounge. Bring a photo ID.
  • Arrive at your VIP check-in time, and keep your wristband on all night.
  • Keep the address private. Don't post it or share it.
  • Ask before you film or post a celebrity guest, and follow their team's lead. We'll set up the meet-and-greet moments.
  • Many festival guests are under 18. Keep minors out of your content unless a parent says OK.
  • Respect "no filming" areas and the family's private spaces.
  • Be kind to staff, security and each other. That's how we all get invited back.
