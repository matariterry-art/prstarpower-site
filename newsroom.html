PR STARPOWER announces representation of Freda Payne

PR STARPOWER has announced its representation of recording artist, actress and author Freda Payne. The two-time gold record artist appeared this year as cover star of both Decree Entertainment Magazine and Courageous Woman Magazine, and was among the most talked-about presences of Oscars week. She continues to perform to sold-out rooms including Catalina Bar & Grill in Hollywood and Blues Alley in Washington. Her full press kit is available here.
