King Larry “The Party God” Announces The Life of Party Tour After a Standout Season in Los Angeles and Las Vegas

The verified artist (350K Instagram followers) is taking his new music on the road.

PR STARPOWER Press Room · For immediate release

King Larry “The Party God.” Photo courtesy of King Larry.

LOS ANGELES, Nov. 20, 2026 — Recording artist King Larry, known across nightlife as “The Party God,” is preparing to launch The Life of Party Tour, with dates and cities to be announced soon and a major artist set to join him on the bill. The tour follows a standout season of promoting his new music in Los Angeles, along with a recent appearance in Las Vegas.

While in town, Larry attended Jamie Foxx’s annual Halloween celebration, a hugely successful night, and worked behind the scenes to help connect artist JhonnieDamnD to the event. He attended with friends including Hazel-E of Love & Hip Hop: Hollywood and K-pop artist Nikki Paige.

“We want bigger and better,” said Larry. “The Life of Party Tour is coming, and we’re going to have a big artist get on there with me. Shout out to Terry and PR Star Power, and to my amazing sponsors.”

Larry’s music, including the single “Emotion Potion,” is available at lsmithmusic.com. Fans can follow the movement on Instagram at @iampartygawddd and in his Team Partyyy community.

About King Larry “The Party God”

King Larry (Larry Smith) is a recording artist and entertainer known in nightlife as “The Party God.” A verified creator with more than 350,000 Instagram followers, he releases music through lsmithmusic.com and is preparing The Life of Party Tour.
